RULES OF AUTHORISED LOTTERIES
1      INTRODUCTION
1.1    These Rules regulate the conduct of public lotteries and Australian Football Soccer Pools by Tatts NT Lotteries Pty
       Ltd pursuant to the Licences in respect of:
       (a)      TattsLotto;
       (b)      Super 66;
       (c)      Oz Lotto;
       (d)      Monday & Wednesday Lotto;
       (e)      Powerball; and
       (f)      The Pools.
1.2    The Rules that are in force at the time of purchase of a Lottery Ticket or Syndicates Share in a Draw of an
       Authorised Lottery are contractually binding on Tatts NT, the player and the Syndicate player.
1.3    These Rules are in force for Draws of Authorised Lotteries conducted on and after 30 July 2012 until subsequent
       amendments are made by Tatts NT.
1.4    These Rules shall be available for inspection at the places of business of Tatts NT, Retailers, Resellers and on the
       Web Site.
1.5    Rules 1 to 20 are the Rules that are common to each of the Authorised Lotteries.
1.6    Schedules A to F contain the Rules that are specific to the particular Authorised Lottery named in the respective
       Schedule.
1.7    If there is an inconsistency between Rules 1 to 20 and any of the Rules contained in Schedules A to F, the Rules
       contained in Schedules A to F will prevail to the extent of any such inconsistency.

3      EFFECT OF THESE RULES
3.1    Entry into Authorised Lotteries is only open to persons 18 years of age or over in the Northern Territory and in
       Overseas Jurisdictions where that law is applicable.
3.2    If:
       (a)    Entries or Syndicate Entries are not available for purchase via any of the distribution methods
              contemplated by these Rules, the relevant Rules will only be operative if and when such purchase is
              available; and
       (b)    particular types or styles of Entries or Syndicate Entries are unavailable for purchase, then the relevant
              Rules will only be operative if and when such types or styles are available.
3.3    By purchasing a Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share in an Authorised Lottery, all players and Syndicate players
       agree to be bound by these Rules. Except as may otherwise be provided herein and in particular Rule 13.2, all
       players and Syndicate players shall accept as final, decisions made by Tatts NT pursuant to these Rules and
       decisions made by Tatts NT as to the meaning and effect of these Rules.
3.4    Tatts NT may at any time amend these Rules in accordance with the Licences or Acts. If such amendment results
       in the invalidity of any Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share already issued, the holder of such Lottery Ticket or
       Syndicate Share may apply for a refund of amount paid for that Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share but Tatts NT
       shall not be liable for any other loss or damage suffered by the player or Syndicate player.
3.5    If there is an inconsistency between the instructions on an Entry Coupon, Lottery Ticket, Syndicate Share or
       promotional and point of sale materials and these Rules, these Rules prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
3.6    If it is held by a court of competent jurisdiction that:
       (a)    any part of the Rules are void or illegal or unenforceable; or
       (b)    the Rules would be void, voidable illegal or unenforceable in whole or in part unless a part were severed
              from these Rules,
       then that part shall be severable from and shall not affect the continued operation of the remainder of the Rules.
3.7    Tatts NT’s lottery system may have various restrictions which affect its ability to:
       (a)    process Entry requests which exceed or are less than a given number of Standard Entries for any one
              Draw; and
       (b)    in the case of System Entries or Pick Entries, process Entry requests which exceed or are less than the
              allowable given number of separate or combined System Entries or Pick Entries or Standard Entries on the
              one Entry Coupon and/or Lottery Ticket.
4      ENTRY COUPONS
4.1    Entry Coupons:
       (a)    may be different for each Authorised Lottery;
       (b)    may contain between 10 and 26 Game Panels (marked in either alphabetical or numerical sequence) for
              number selections by players;
       (c)    can be used to select from the following types of Entries (if available for a particular Authorised Lottery):
              (i)     a Standard Entry;
              (ii)    a System Entry;
              (iii)   a Pick Entry;
              (iv)    PowerHit or Play the Field (Powerball only)
              and the player may elect to play that Entry type (if available for the chosen Authorised Lottery):
              (v)     in Advance; and/or
              (vi)    for Multi-weeks; and/or

               by completing or marking the Entry Coupon in accordance with the instructions appearing on the Entry
               Coupon with each Entry costing the Subscription plus Commission printed on the Lottery Ticket or Share
               Price if in respect of a Syndicate Share and advertised by Tatts NT for the relevant Authorised Lottery at
               the time and point of purchase;
       (d)     must be marked by hand (and by no other means unless first approved by Tatts NT in writing);

               player.
4.2    Only an Entry Coupon that is provided or approved by Tatts NT shall be capable of constituting a properly
       completed Entry Coupon and notwithstanding any other provision contained in these Rules, a Prize may not be
       paid to any player whose Entry emanates from an Entry Coupon that is not provided or approved by Tatts NT.
5      QUICK PICK AND NON-COUPON ENTRIES
5.1    A player may request, by verbal or electronic instruction not requiring completion of an Entry Coupon, a Quick
       Pick Entry and nominate any of the following types of Entries (if available for a particular Authorised Lottery):
       (i)     a Standard Entry;
       (ii)    a System Entry;
       (iii)   a Pick Entry;
       (iv)    PowerHit or Play the Field (Powerball only)
       and the player may elect to play that Entry type (if available for the chosen Authorised Lottery):
       (v)     in Advance; and/or
       (vi)    for Multi-weeks
       whereupon the Lottery Ticket will contain numbers randomly selected by Tatts NT’s lottery system.
5.2    An Entry properly transacted via the Web Site or a Reseller in accordance with Rules 18 and 19 respectively shall
       constitute an Entry in accordance with these Rules.
5.3    Any reference to Entry Coupons and the marking of Entry Coupons in Rule 4 is not applicable to Quick Pick
       Entries, Syndicate Shares and Entries purchased via the Web Site or Resellers.
6      SYNDICATE ENTRIES
6.1    A Syndicate may be formed for each of the Authorised Lotteries or a combination of each and any of the
       Authorised Lotteries by:
       (a)     Tatts NT;
       (b)     a Retailer;
       (c)     a group of 2 or more Retailers.
       and each will be known as a “Syndicate Organiser”.
6.2    A Syndicate Organiser forms a Syndicate by:
       (a)     registering the following for the Syndicate on Tatts NT’s lottery system:
               (i)     the Authorised Lottery or Authorised Lotteries to which the Syndicate relates;
               (ii)    for a Syndicate formed by a Retailer or a group of 2 or more Retailers, the location or identity of
                       the Retailer’s outlet where the Syndicate Shares will be available for sale;
               (iii)   for a Syndicate formed by a Retailer or a group of 2 or more Retailers, the number of Syndicate
                       Shares each Retailer is responsible to sell; and
       (b)     selecting the following for the Syndicate from the options available on Tatts NT’s lottery system:
               (i)     the type of Entry, or combination of types of Entries, in the Authorised Lottery or combination of
                       Authorised Lotteries;
               (ii)    the number of Syndicate Shares in the Syndicate.
6.3    If a Syndicate Share is not sold 10 minutes prior to the Close of Acceptance of Entries for the first Draw applicable
       to that Syndicate Entry, the Syndicate Share is automatically issued to:

7      ISSUING LOTTERY TICKETS
7.1    A player having made a selection and paid the Subscription plus Commission shall be issued with a Lottery Ticket
       recording:
       (a)    the name of the relevant Authorised Lottery;
       (b)    the player’s Player Card number and name, if applicable;
       (c)    the selected numbers and if applicable, the type of Entry;
       (d)    the Draw number and the respective Draw date(s), or if a Multi-week Entry, the Draw date ranges and
              Draw number ranges, to which the Entry relates;
       (e)    if applicable, “e” which indicates that the operator of the Lottery Terminal has edited the selection at the
              player’s direction;
       (f)    if applicable, “t” which indicates that the selection is a “Top Up”;
       (g)    if applicable, “^” which indicates that the selection is a PowerHit entry;
       (h)    details of the Retailer and relevant selling Lottery Terminal;
       (i)    the date and time the Entry is processed by Tatts NT’s lottery system via a Lottery Terminal;
       (j)    the Subscription and Commission paid and their total; and
       (k)    the number of the Lottery Ticket;
8      ISSUING SYNDICATE SHARES
8.1    A Syndicate player having made a request to participate in a Syndicate Entry and paid the Share Price will be
       issued with a Syndicate Share recording:
       (a)    the name of the relevant Authorised Lottery or Authorised Lotteries;
       (b)    the player’s Player Card number and name, if applicable;
       (c)    the selected numbers in each of the relevant Authorised Lotteries and if applicable, the type of Entry(s);
       (d)    the Draw number and the respective Draw date(s), or if a Multi-week Entry, the Draw date ranges and
              Draw number ranges, to which the Syndicate Entry relates;
       (e)    if applicable, “^” which indicates that the selection is a PowerHit entry;
       (f)    the details of the Retailer and relevant selling Lottery Terminal;
       (g)    the date and time the Syndicate Share is processed by Tatts NT’s lottery system via a Lottery Terminal;
       (h)    the Subscription and Commission paid for the Syndicate Entry and their total;
       (i)    the number of Syndicate Shares in the Syndicate;
       (j)    the Syndicate number and name;
       (k)    the Share Price; and
       (l)    the ticket number of the Syndicate Share.

9      ACCEPTANCE OF LOTTERY TICKETS AND SYNDICATE SHARES
9.1    It is the responsibility of the player or Syndicate player to check the accuracy of all details on the Lottery Ticket or
       Syndicate Share at the time it is received by the player or Syndicate player and the taking or acceptance of a
       Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share by a player or Syndicate player constitutes an acknowledgment by the player or
       Syndicate Player that the numbers printed thereon are the player’s selection (whether by Coupon Entry or Quick
       Pick) and that all other particulars and details on the Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share are correct.
9.2    Subject to Rule 3.4, a Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share may only be voided or cancelled,
       (a)    on presentation of the original Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share which must be legible and must not be
              mutilated; and
       (b)    at the place where the Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share was sold; and
       (c)    prior to the Close of Acceptance of Entries for the first Draw applicable to that Entry or Syndicate Entry
              respectively and subject to the relevant Retailer’s hours of operation,
       (d)    by Tatts NT, in Tatts NT absolute discretion, if requested by a Retailer on its own or on behalf of a player
              or Syndicate player and subject to the capability of Tatts NT’s lottery system
       and upon cancellation the player or Syndicate player shall be refunded the Subscription and Commission paid in
       respect of such Lottery Ticket or the Syndicate Share Fee if in respect of a Syndicate Share.
9.3    A Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share which is recorded as void or cancelled on or in Tatts NT’s lottery system shall
       be void regardless of whether such Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share was cancelled in error by a Retailer or Tatts
       NT or otherwise, and no Prize shall be payable in respect of such Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share.
9.4    It is the responsibility of the player or Syndicate player to ensure that the correct Lottery Ticket or Syndicate
       Share has been cancelled and to inform the Retailer or Tatts NT if there has been an error in relation to
       cancellation of the Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share.
9.5    Neither the Retailer nor Tatts NT shall be liable to the player or Syndicate Player in respect of an error in the
       cancellation of a Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share.
9.6    In the event that the details recorded on the player's or Syndicate player's Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share are
       not consistent with the particulars and details recorded on Tatts NT’s lottery system then the latter shall apply to
       the exclusion of the former and shall determine what Prize, if any, the player or Syndicate player shall be entitled
       to and the player or Syndicate player shall be bound by any such determination.
9.7    Subject to these Rules, a Lottery Ticket or Syndicate Share issued in accordance with these Rules shall constitute
       an acknowledgment by Tatts NT of such Entry or Syndicate Share in the relevant Draw of an Authorised Lottery.

2      OBJECTIVE

2.1    The objective of the player participating in The Pools is to correctly select the Winning Numbers from the numbers
       1 to 38 inclusive in any one Standard Entry.

3      DETERMINATION OF RESULTS

3.1    Subject to Rule 3.3 of this Schedule, the Matches (represented by the numbers in a Game Panel) shall be deemed
       to have an order of rank depending on the results of those Matches in accordance with this Rule and for the
       purposes of Rule 4 of this Schedule, the six highest ranked numbered Matches shall be the Winning Numbers and
       the seventh highest ranked numbered Match will be the Supplementary Number:-
       (a)    Score Draw - All Score Draws will be ranked higher than any other result and a Score Draw where more
              goals are scored will be ranked higher than a Score Draw where fewer goals are scored.
       (b)    Nil Score Draw (scoreless) - All Nil Score Draws will be ranked equally and will be ranked higher than an
              Away Win or a Home Win.
       (c)    Win for Away Team - All Wins for Away Teams shall be ranked higher than Home Wins. Away Wins with a
              smaller goal difference shall be ranked higher than Away Wins with greater goal difference and where two
              or more Away Wins have the same goal difference Away Wins where more goals are scored shall be
              ranked higher than Away Wins where fewer goals are scored.
       (d)    Win for Home Team - Home Wins with a smaller goal difference shall be ranked higher than Home Wins
              with greater goal difference and where two or more Home Wins have the same goal difference Home Wins
              where more goals are scored shall be ranked higher than Home Wins where fewer goals are scored.
       (e)    In the event that two or more numbered Matches in a Game Panel are of equal rank in accordance with
              Rules 3.1(a) through 3.1(d) inclusive of this Schedule, then any such Match printed with a greater number
              (in magnitude) in a game panel shall be ranked higher than any other such Match printed with a lesser
              number (in magnitude).
       (f)    If there are insufficient results in accordance with Rule 3.1(a) of this Schedule to determine the Winning
              Numbers and the Supplementary Number, then the results in accordance with Rule 3.1(b) of this Schedule
              shall be taken into account and if still insufficient then the results in accordance with Rule 3.1(c) of this
              Schedule shall be taken into account and if still insufficient then the results in accordance with Rule 3.1(d)
              of this Schedule shall be taken into account.
3.2    References in Rules 3.1(c) and 3.1(d) of this Schedule to “more goals” or “fewer goals” refers to the total number
       of goals scored by both teams in a Match result.
3.3    In the event that any numbered Match in a Game Panel is a Void Match, the result of such Void Match will be
       deemed to be the result of the first non-void numbered Match on the List of Matches from number 39 onwards. A
       second such Void Match will be the result of the second non-void numbered Match on the List of Matches from
       number 39 onwards and so on in ascending numerical sequence for each such Void Match. The ranking of any
       such reserve Match will be determined in accordance with Rule 3.1.
3.4    If in any Draw less than 38 Matches remain scheduled to be played immediately prior to the Close of Acceptance
       of Entries, all entries received in accordance with these Rules in respect of that Draw shall be cancelled and
       automatically resubmitted as entries in the next occurring Draw.
3.5    In the event that there are insufficient Match results (including those in respect of reserve Matches substituted for
       Voided Matches) to determine the six winning numbers and the supplementary number, a Reserve Draw will be
       conducted on the Monday following the conclusion of the relevant Draw whereby:
       (a)    the balls to be used initially will be numbered from 1 to 38; and
       (b)    the numbered balls corresponding to the winning numbers already determined in accordance with Rule 3.1
              of this Schedule will be removed and the Reserve Draw will be conducted with only those remaining
              numbered balls; and
       (c)    the number of balls required to be drawn to constitute the full complement of six winning numbers and
              one supplementary number will be drawn.
3.6    Any such Reserve Draw shall be conducted under the supervision of a nominated or approved representative of
       the Victorian Government and shall be conducted in accordance with the requirements of the relevant regulatory
       body for the jurisdiction in which the Draw is conducted. The representative of the Victorian Government will
       record the numbers drawn in drawn order, and the numbers so drawn shall be added to the numbers already
       determined in accordance with Rule 3.1 of this Schedule, in drawn order sequence, until six winning Match
       numbers and a supplementary Match number are determined.
3.7    In the event of a Malfunction in the conduct of a Reserve Draw, Rules 10.2, 10.3 and 10.4 will apply.

4      DIVISION OF PRIZES

4.1    There shall be five (5) Prize divisions in each Draw as follows:


       Division One:        Six Winning Numbers in any one Standard Entry;
       Division Two:        Five of the six Winning Numbers plus the Supplementary Number in any one Standard
                            Entry;
       Division Three:      Five of the six Winning Numbers in any one Standard Entry;
       Division Four:       Four of the six Winning Numbers in any one Standard Entry;
       Division Five:       Three of the six Winning Numbers, plus the Supplementary Number, in any one Standard
                            Entry.


4.2    If in a particular Draw the prize payable for a single winning Standard Entry in:
       (a)    Divisions Two, Three or Four is less than the prize payable to a winning Entry in any lower Division, the
              amounts allocated to that Division and all lower Divisions will be aggregated and divided equally between
              all the winning entry Games in all the Divisions whose allocated prize pools were aggregated;
       (b)    any Division is less than $1.00, that prize shall be increased so that the amount payable is $1.00.

       Division Two:        2% of the Prize Fund
       Division Three:      6% of the Prize Fund
       Division Four:       15% of the Prize Fund
       Division Five:       12% of the Prize Fund
