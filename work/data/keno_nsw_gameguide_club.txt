Your chance to win over
$1 million every 3 minutes
Keno gives you the chance to win over
$1 million every 3 minutes by simply picking
numbers you think will be drawn from the Keno
ball draw.

Play and match your
numbers to win
Pick how many numbers you’d like to play,
choosing 1 - 10, 15, 20 or 40 numbers.
20 numbers are drawn from the 80 available on
the Keno game grid. Match the numbers you play
to the numbers drawn for your chance to win over
$1 million. You can play Keno for one or more
games.
The more games you play, the greater chance
you have of winning.

Don’t match all your numbers?
You could still be a winner!
Keno gives everyone the chance to be a winner.
Even when you don’t match all of your numbers,
you could still be in with a chance to win a prize.

How to win over $1 million
To win the $1 million jackpot, you must win the 10
Spot jackpot. To win the 10 Spot, play 10 numbers
and match all of these numbers.

Keno Advanced
A more powerful way to play.
Keno Advanced lets you play groups of
numbers that form multiple combinations
on one ticket. Keno Advanced is available
to play on Keno Classic and Keno Mega
Millions.

Groups of numbers are formed by
marking numbers alphabetically, starting
with the letter A (instead of the usual X).
Each group of numbers represents one
single entry.

Multiple groups of numbers can be
combined to form additional entries on the
one ticket.

For example, if 4 numbers are marked
with the letter A, and 4 numbers are
marked with the letter B, groups A & B
can be combined to form an 8 Spot entry
(8 numbers).

The minimum cost per combination
depends on the number of combinations
played. See table below for details.

    Number of            Minimum combination          Minimum combination
   combinations           cost Keno Classic          cost Keno Mega Millions


       50+                       10¢                           20¢
     20-49                       20¢                           40¢
       4-19                      50¢                             $1
        1-3                        $1                           $2

 Minimum bet per combination for Jackpots is $1 on Keno Classic, $2 on Keno Mega
Millions and minimum ticket cost is $1 on Keno Classic and $2 on Keno Mega Millions.

   Know your odds
   Keno is conducted under a licence issued by the
   State and as such, must comply with the appropriate
   legislation governing the operation of the game. Players
   must be at least 18 years of age to participate in a game
   of Keno. Total ticket value must equal at least $1, or $2
   for Keno Mega Millions.
   Keno Agents have set payout limits, which determine
   the amount of Keno winnings to be paid as cash to the
   customer, with any balance paid via cheque.
   Game results are available from the Keno Agent or
   online at keno.com.au. Please also check the Keno
   game screens available at all Keno Agents for game
   results.
   When a ticket is presented for payment on the day of
   purchase, any outstanding unplayed games will be
   cancelled and refunded.
   Winnings are paid on the presentation of the printed
   receipt ticket only. It is your responsibility to check your
   Keno ticket for completeness and accuracy before the
   commencement of the game as the payment of wins is
   based upon the numbers on the printed receipt ticket.
   The Keno operator is not liable for a player’s failure to
   check their Keno receipt ticket.

   What are your jackpot chances?
   On games where you’re playing 7 to 10 numbers,
   you’re playing to win a jackpot. If every number you
   choose comes up, you’ll win the jackpot. The jackpots
   increase with each game until they are won. Check out
   Keno game screens for current jackpot amounts.

Responsible Gambling and
Gambling Support Services
Customers who play Keno are encouraged to
set time and money limits according to their
circumstances and to not exceed that limit. Keno’s
Responsible Gambling Framework and Responsible
Gambling Codes of Conduct are available on:
https://www.tabcorp.com.au/sustainability/
responsible-gambling
Are you experiencing any of the
behaviours below?
- Gambling every day or finding it hard to stop at
  closing time
- Gambling for long periods, without taking a break
- Avoiding contact with others while gambling
- Trying to borrow money from staff or other
  customers
- Continuing to gamble with the proceeds of large
  wins
- Aggressive, anti-social or emotional behaviour
  while gambling
- Gambling to win back losses
You may be having difficulties and need support in
managing your gambling activities.
Support is available by calling:
Gambling Help on 1800 858 858 or visiting
www.gamblinghelponline.nsw.gov.au
