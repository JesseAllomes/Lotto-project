# Site data audit (Sonnet): 42-competition sample

Audited 2026-10-31 ~18:15 AEDT. Method: every `open_button_url` and `listing_page` fetched and rendered with Playwright (scripts only, no entry forms touched, no bot checks bypassed); evidence quotes are from the rendered promoter page unless marked 'T&C summary' (the FAQ on the aggregator listing, which restates the promoter T&Cs and is used only where the promoter page was blocked or silent). Chance verdicts are my judgement, not measurement: popularity signals available were thin (competitions.com.au shows no entry counts or comments; AusComps showed '0 comments'), so I used brand size, number of prizes, entry gating and how long the draw is open.

Sample: 42 comps (competitions.com.au 40, auscomps.au 2). `prize_is_estimate` was false on all 42, so no estimate could be judged.

## (c) Systematic problems and pipeline fixes (most impactful first)

**1. Unknown prize value is written as `prize_shown = 0` with `prize_is_estimate = false` (11 of 42 rows, 26%).**
Affected: 76075, 72033, 75907, 76110, 75449, 72517, 76188, 74216, 76173, 10007759, 75489. All are physical or digital items whose promoter gives no dollar figure (DVDs, a pantry pack, film passes, a bag, a freezer of food). A $0 prize is a false statement and probably drags these rows into the wrong chance band and sort order.
Fix: store `prize_value = null` (never 0) and fill an estimate with `prize_is_estimate = true` from title and prize text (count x typical unit price, e.g. 5 DVDs ~ $125, 5 digital movie passes ~ $100, Coach Tabby ~ $600). Render null as "Value not stated", and make the chance formula treat null as 'unknown', not 'worthless'.

**2. Prize value taken from a headline ceiling, a per-unit price or a sum over several draws (4 wrong, plus 4 more ceilings).**
- "Up to" ceilings: Beatbot shows $23,994 (= 6 x the $3,999 top robot) but the six listed draws sum to $13,594; Converse $2,300 is the top of a tiered $600-$1,800 + $500 bonus ladder; Disney+ $33,250 and Coopers $9,654 are sums of "up to" components.
- Top-envelope prizes: SmartMoney "$500,000" is one of 50 envelopes in each of two groups (about 4% to hit it), consolation $1,000, so the headline overstates the prize roughly 24x relative to expected value.
- Per-unit shown for a multi-prize comp: Taiter Tots shows $129 for "1 of 4" ($129.95 each = $519.80).
- Sum of 10 draws attached to one pack: Yarn shows $4,950 while the page's pack is $521.60.
Fix: parse T&C/FAQ for patterns `up to`, `envelope`, `each`, `per winner`, `tier`, `choose one of`, `Draw N ... Worth $X`. Store `value_min`, `value_likely`, `value_max`, `n_prizes`, and set `prize_shown = n_prizes x per-unit` when "each" is present, `sum(listed draws)` when draws are itemised, and the guaranteed/consolation value (not the jackpot) for envelope draws. Show "up to" in the UI when it is a ceiling.

**3. `prizes_counted` undercounts multi-winner structures, so 'low' bands are too pessimistic (7 understated chance rows).**
Affected: Yarn (10 pack draws, entrant is in all), Australia Unfiltered (~13 prizes: 2 x $2,000 + $2,000 People's Choice + ~10 monthly $100 vouchers), Dreamstreet (winners drawn per home game, ~14), Nike (T&Cs: first two valid entries = 2 winners although title says "a pair"), La Trobe (3 cards + 4 alumni packs), Hercules ("1 IN 100 WINS THE BAG": 1% per entry, instant win, no cap on bags), Interflora.
Fix: extract winner count from T&Cs with the phrases `N winners`, `first two valid entries`, `each month/week/game`, `Draw 1..N`, `in every draw`, `1 in N`; multiply by the number of draws; for instant-win/odds-stated promos, use the stated odds directly instead of `prizes / assumed entrants`.

**4. The chance band ignores entry gating and audience size, and the aggregator's boilerplate "free to enter, no purchase necessary" is copied to every row.**
Examples: Interflora entry is a Google review of a genuine Interflora order (purchase-linked, so far fewer entrants: real band high, shown medium); Tentworld auto-enters the entire existing subscriber list and runs monthly (shown medium, should be low); I Love Ugly PS5 Pro + GTA VI at a big streetwear brand (shown medium, should be low); ShadyBaby (MoM, 2 prizes) shown high while the sibling Snotty Noses (MoM, 2 prizes) is medium; Disney+ needs a paid subscription plus enrolment; La Trobe is alumni-only; Nike needs membership + app workout; Converse needs referrals; Harvey Norman, Mindfood and MoM need judged 25-50 word answers; Australia Unfiltered needs photos.
Fix: add structured gating fields from T&Cs (`needs_purchase`, `needs_membership`, `needs_login`, `skill_or_judged`, `referral_required`, `existing_list_auto_entered`, `entry_actions`) and use them as multipliers on expected entrants (purchase/membership/skill/referral shrink the pool; auto-entered mailing lists and "hot" prizes such as consoles, iPhones and trips enlarge it). Stop displaying "no purchase necessary" unless the T&Cs say so. Use brand size (comps run on the aggregator, social following) as an audience proxy: 633 MoM comps and 687 Beauty Heaven comps signal big lists, 1-3 comps signal small ones.

**5. Link problems: 5 of 33 checkable links are bad, 9 more could not be checked, and `link_flag` caught only 1 of the 5.**
- Dead: Dream Queen `/pages/win-a-coach-tabby` 301s to `/pages/vibe-check-quiz` which is 404 (Shopify redirect to a deleted page); Limelight `/competitions/win-the-elders-book/` is 404.
- Home page: fropro.com.au home (no giveaway text); Disney+ Perks landing (`en_au/welcome` redirected to `en_us/welcome`, no Disney World promo visible).
- Circular: Salty Johnson Open button = the aggregator's own page (no "Opens on <site>" line); the flag did fire here.
- Several JS pages show nothing in a plain fetch (Calexico, Infamous Swim, Tentworld, komo.site form) but are correct once rendered, so a non-rendering checker will wrongly flag good links.
Fix in the link checker: (i) render with a headless browser, follow redirects and record the final URL and final HTTP status, then mark dead if final status is 404/410 or text matches `not found|404`; (ii) mark home page when the final path is `/` or when the page text lacks both the brand's giveaway keywords and any 3 tokens from the title/prize (use the same "page names neither this brand nor this prize" test that already produced the Salty Johnson flag, but run it on every row); (iii) mark circular when the open URL host equals the listing host; (iv) when the check is blocked (Cloudflare/Imperva/403/captcha) store `link_status = blocked`, not correct. Note the Mouths of Mums 403s are an IP ban from this audit environment, so 'blocked' means unverified, not wrong.

**6. Close dates: rolling draws get invented end dates, multi-draw promos use the wrong end, and times are defaulted.**
- Ongoing monthly draws shown with a fixed end: Ozganics 30 Mar 2027 and FroPro 31 Dec 2026 (both pages and the aggregator's own FAQ say no end date); Tentworld is also a monthly draw.
- Wrong time: SmartMoney closes 2:30 PM on 17 Dec (terms: `closes 17/12/26 at 2:30 PM`), shown 11:59pm.
- Wrong date: Yarn pack closes about 8 Nov (page countdown + 'Drawn Sun 8 Nov'), shown 11 Nov.
- Unstated times are displayed as 11:59pm in the UI (all `close_time_shown = 11:59pm` rows without a time on the promoter page).
- AEST/AEDT: T&Cs written as "AEST" during daylight saving (Coopers, La Trobe: 11:59 PM AEST 30 Nov; Mindfood 10 PM AEST 8 Nov; Beatbot 11:00 AM AEST 1 Dec) are an hour later in Melbourne; the AWST conversions (ALYN, Dream Queen) were done correctly, so extend that to AEST-in-DST.
Fix: if the page or FAQ says `ongoing|every month|monthly draw` and no date, set `close = null`, `recurring = true`, and show "Monthly draw" with the next draw date; keep the time null when unknown and show date only; when a promo has several packs/draws, take the end date of the specific draw the Open URL points to; parse the T&C close clause (`closes ... at HH:MM`) in preference to a default; map the stated zone via `zoneinfo` using the date (AEST in DST means UTC+10).

**7. Category is inherited from the aggregator's tag or brand, not from the prize (8 of 42).**
Corrections: FroPro "freezer full of food" Home & garden -> Food & drink; RLWC prize pack Cash & vouchers -> Tickets & experiences ($3.5k of $4.6k is tickets/experience); Paw Patrol digital rental passes Tickets & experiences -> Books & media; ALYN smart rings Fashion & beauty -> Tech & gaming; Beatbot pool robots Tech & gaming -> Home & garden; Hercules gym bag Fashion & beauty -> Other; Tentworld camping fridge Home & garden -> Other; Michelle Mitchell book packs Kids & baby -> Books & media.

**8. Scrape/parse defects in brand and title fields.**
AusComps rows: brand shows the literal placeholder "Text" (10006281, from the page's "Text x Text" header) and is empty for 10007759. Brand is the publisher, not the sponsor, on several rows (ReviewBrisbane/Russell Hobbs, Mouths of Mums, Russh/Koala, Disney+ Perks). Titles are garbled by the pipeline's "Win 1 of N" prefix: "Win 1 of 5 One of Five Grin DVDs", "Win 1 of 6 Winning Parenting Educator Michelle Mitchell" (prize is books), "Family Passes" vs T&Cs "double passes" (unverified).

**9. Coverage gap: 8 of 42 (19%) promoter pages are behind bot protection (5 Mouths of Mums, Converse, Mindfood, Gleam via wn.nr), plus 1 Facebook login wall and Zyft's Gleam entry widget.**
This is a monitoring limitation rather than an error in the data, but 19% of rows cannot be re-verified by a plain collector, and MoM alone is 633 comps on the aggregator.
Fix: for blocked hosts, record `link_status = blocked` and rely on the aggregator's T&C summary for value/close (it matched the promoter on every row where both were readable: Nike, Tentworld, Disney arithmetic, Taiter Tots slug, ShadyBaby slug), but mark those fields `source = aggregator_tc`; schedule a low-frequency browser check from a residential IP for the blocked hosts.

## Notes on method and limits

- Link verdict 'correct' means the rendered page is this competition's entry page or its terms; entry widgets behind Gleam/Cloudflare (Zyft) were not opened.
- Chance verdicts are judgement calls (no entry counts were visible anywhere); the most solid ones are those backed by a stated odds or gating fact: Hercules 1-in-100, Interflora review-of-order, Tentworld auto-entry, SmartMoney envelopes, Yarn 10 draws, Australia Unfiltered prize list.
- Summary error rates exclude rows that could not be checked; they are in the right-hand column of the summary table.
- Raw rendered pages and scripts are in the session scratchpad (`aud_pages/`, `aud_fetch.js`), not in the project.
